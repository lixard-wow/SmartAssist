# SmartAssist

Never edit your Misdirection, Tricks of the Trade or Power Infusion macro again. SmartAssist builds the macro for you and keeps it pointed at the right player, switching to your tank automatically when you join a group.

- **Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/mark-of-the-tank)
- **User guide:** [lixard-wow.github.io/smart-assist](https://lixard-wow.github.io/smart-assist/)
- **Game:** Retail, Midnight
- **Classes:** Hunter (Misdirection), Rogue (Tricks of the Trade) and Priest (Power Infusion).

## Features
- Builds one macro for your spell, with the spell's icon, and keeps it up to date.
- Points the macro at your tank when you join a group or the group changes.
- Hover the minimap button to switch the macro to your tank, target, target of target, focus or pet in one click.
- Falls back to your pet, then to a normal cast, if the chosen player isn't there.
- Pops up the quick menu when you zone in with a group and no target set.
- Changes made in combat are applied as soon as combat ends.

## Getting started
1. Install the addon and log in on your Hunter, Rogue or Priest.
2. Open your Macros window (`/macro`) and drag the new macro onto your action bar.
3. Join a group. The macro now points at your tank.

The addon needs one free general macro slot.

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/smartassist` | Open settings (right-clicking the minimap button also works) |
